Your trust matters

Privacy & important information

Last updated: 19 November 2026

MamaNestLe respects your privacy. This page explains how information shared through our website and WhatsApp enquiries is intended to be handled, and provides important information about the nature of content on this website.

1. Information you choose to share

If you submit the website enquiry form, the details you provide (name, email address, enquiry type and optional message) are sent to a private Google Sheet managed by MamaNestLe through a Google Apps Script web app. The Sheet is intended to be accessible only to authorised MamaNestLe administrators. You may also choose to contact us separately through WhatsApp.

2. How we use enquiry information

We use the information you voluntarily send to understand your enquiry, respond to you, communicate relevant updates you have requested and improve our understanding of the maternal-wellbeing needs people raise with us. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Information to avoid sharing

Please do not send medical records, test reports, identity documents, account passwords or detailed sensitive health information through this website or an introductory WhatsApp enquiry. If a future service requires specific information, the collection process and applicable safeguards should be explained separately before you provide it.

4. Storage, access and retention

Messages sent through WhatsApp are processed by WhatsApp in accordance with its own terms and privacy policy. MamaNestLe should limit access to enquiry conversations to people who need the information to respond. Enquiry information should be retained only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the enquiry, follow-up or applicable legal requirements, and then securely deleted. Enquiry records are stored in a private Google Sheet. Access should be restricted to authorised MamaNestLe administrators and records should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the enquiry, follow-up or applicable legal requirements.

5. Cookies and third-party services

This website uses Google Fonts to display typography and GitHub Pages to host the website. These providers may process technical information such as browser requests and IP addresses under their own policies. The website does not currently use advertising pixels or behavioural analytics by design.

6. Your choices and requests

You may choose what information to share. You can ask us to correct or delete enquiry information we hold, subject to applicable legal obligations and information that must be retained. Contact us through our official WhatsApp number listed on the home page.

7. Website content and health-related information

Content on this website is intended for general information and awareness. It is not individual medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and does not replace consultation with a qualified healthcare professional. For personal health concerns, urgent symptoms or decisions about your care or your baby's care, please contact an appropriately qualified healthcare professional or emergency service.

Any areas of maternal support described on this website communicate MamaNestLe's focus and the kinds of enquiries we welcome. The scope, availability and delivery of any specific service will be discussed and confirmed directly before an arrangement is made. Please do not assume that an enquiry alone creates a professional or clinical relationship.

8. Updates to this page

We may update this page as our website, enquiry process and services evolve. The latest version will always be published here with its revision date.

For privacy questions or requests, contact MamaNestLe via WhatsApp: +91 81789 03102.
